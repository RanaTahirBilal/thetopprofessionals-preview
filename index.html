How a profile here is made

  • 01 Every statement carries a source A profile is assembled one fact at a time, each recorded against a document with the date an editor read it. Nothing is written from memory.
  • 02 Disputed claims are refused Where two sources disagree, or where a source has no retrieval date, the claim is held back. It does not appear in softer wording.
  • 03 Consent is on record first No profile is published without the subject agreeing to it, and no release is issued in a person’s name without their agreement to that too.
  • 04 Corrections go back to the source A correction is checked against the document the sentence rested on, not settled by argument. The record changes or the claim goes.
